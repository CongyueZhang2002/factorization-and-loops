# CF303 own physical soft modes

Date: 2026-09-03

## Result

The four genuinely new CF303 source modes are now represented by an exact,
compact endpoint record.  In the accepted 43-source ordering their embedding
is

```text
V_own(34,1) = 1
V_own(34,2) = 1,  V_own(35,2) = -2
V_own(36,3) = 1
V_own(37,4) = 1.
```

Accepted source coordinates `{34,35,36,37}` are state rows
`{40,41,42,43}` and original CF303 masters `{3,4,1,2}`.  The normalized
normal-residue eigenvalues are `{0,-4,0,0}`; the corresponding connection
exponents are `{0,-4 eps,0,0}`.  The exact formulas, ordering, and sparse
embedding are in `Artifacts/CF303OwnPhysicalSoftModes.wl`.

The physical transformation is `I_source=(TDiagonal.S) F_canonical`, but it
must be applied to the complete canonical Frobenius solution, including its
regular prefactor `H(rho)`.  Applying it only to a constant residue
eigenvector is not a physical mode map: negative powers in `TDiagonal.S` can
multiply the first normal jets of `H` and cancel apparent leading terms.  An
earlier version of this report incorrectly called those raw transformation
valuations physical-mode valuations; those records have been removed from
the accepted artifact.

The exact direct physical six-master system is the authoritative physical
endpoint record.  Its normal spectrum is `{0,0,0,0,0,-2-4 eps}` and is
handled in `CF303SixModeEndpointFrame.wl` without inferring physical
valuations from a truncated canonical vector.

The cancellation was checked explicitly on the compact block-23 system.
For the canonical zero-mode seed `(1,0)`, the recurrence for the regular
prefactor gives

```text
H1[2] = eps(2p^2-1)/(2p^3(1+4eps)).
```

The raw `rho^-1` term in physical master 4 and the term generated by
`T[-2].H1` are exact opposites.  Including the next jet gives a finite
physical vector with ratio

```text
I4/I3 = (2+5eps)/(2p^2),
```

which is exactly the zero eigenvector of the direct physical residue.  The
nonzero canonical mode keeps its leading singular term and acquires the
integer shift `-4eps -> -2-4eps`.  This proves, rather than assumes, the
spectral reconciliation.

In the complete accepted final-layer deck, only state source column 41
(accepted source coordinate 35) couples to the two final rows.  The exact
base-channel residue is the same in both target rows,

```text
b(eps) = -28(-1+2eps)(-2+3eps)(-1+3eps) /
         (eps^2(2+3eps)).
```

For the source eigenvector `(1,-2)` with eigenvalue `-4 eps`, the induced
target component is therefore `b(eps)/(2 eps) (1,1)`.  The three own zero
source modes have no target component.  Inherited source periods remain a
separate inhomogeneous particular contribution.

## Retraction of the provisional canonical tangential artifact

The earlier `CF303SoftRhoLazyKernels.wl` artifact is removed.  Its extraction
replaced positive `Sqrt[Delta]` syntax but failed to replace inverse and other
odd half-integer powers of the same discriminants.  A decisive symptom was
18 nonzero entries in `[N_soft,B_soft]`, all in state rows 42--43, despite a
`p`-independent residue; this violates the flatness relation for a regular
tangential finite part.  The provisional full-source Gamma and its claimed
kernel census must not be used.

The 35-row recursive source closure itself was sound and exactly invariant,
but it is unnecessary for the homogeneous own-mode map.  Full canonical
Gamma reconstruction was therefore abandoned in favor of the direct
physical six-master route and projection before reconstruction.
