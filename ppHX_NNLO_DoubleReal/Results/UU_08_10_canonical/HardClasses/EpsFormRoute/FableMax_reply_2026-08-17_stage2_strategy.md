Convention (upper-triangular; transpose if yours is lower): with diagonals Δ = εδ (dlog, constant residues) and strip B, the unipotent gauge T = 1 + D gives B′ = B + DΔ_j − Δ_i D − dD. Grade in ε (finite Laurent range, measured): at each order
dD_k = B_k + D_{k−1}δ_j − δ_i D_{k−1} + (known shorter-strip sources) − B′_k.
Process strips by increasing block distance so the equation stays linear with known sources. Three facts make this a closed-form, choice-free procedure:

1. Lowest order is closed. Flatness gives dB = ε(δ_i∧B + B∧δ_j), so at the lowest ε-order dB_{k0} = 0 exactly. A closed rational 1-form on the chart plane minus divisors decomposes as Σ_a c_a dlog φ_a + dR with constant c_a (H¹ of the complement is spanned by the dlogs of the irreducible components). Assign the dlog part to B′_{k0}; the remainder has zero residues everywhere and R is computed entrywise by univariate partial-fraction integration in x, with ∂_y-consistency automatic from flatness.

An ε-deformed locus can never be a letter; a nonzero residue there would put log(φ_ε) into the true solution at some ε-order, contradicting the regularity your assembly certificate already encodes. Q2 — the cycling is a known artifact with a known cure, and you guessed it
