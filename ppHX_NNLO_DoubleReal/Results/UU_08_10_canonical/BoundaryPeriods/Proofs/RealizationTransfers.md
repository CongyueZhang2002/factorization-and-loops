# The twelve realization transfers — exact verification

Codex's assessment (section 3, end) left the twelve listed transfers
"unchecked until their changes of variables and normalizations are verified
exactly". All twelve are now verified exactly.

Verified by `../Scripts/verify_transfers.wls`; result data in
`../Certificates/TransferResults.wl`. Expected acceptance:
`TRANSFERS_CHECKED = 12`, `TRANSFERS_EXACT = 12`,
`CONVENTIONS_UNIFORM = True`.

## The criterion

Each of these periods is a 3-particle massless cut phase-space integral of
the same total momentum `P = ka+kb-kc`, carrying exactly one uncut
denominator. The cut measure `dPhi3(P; q1,q2,q3)` is **symmetric** in the
three cut momenta. Therefore such an integral is completely determined by
the invariant form of its uncut denominator in terms of the cut momenta:

```
PID 6/7 signature :  D = (ka + kb - q)^2      for some cut momentum q
PID 1   signature :  D = (kc - ka + q)^2      for some cut momentum q
```

A transfer to another realization is exact when, in the target family, some
master has index `1` on precisely the three cut propagators and on exactly
one uncut propagator, and that uncut propagator reduces **on the cut** to
the same invariant form. The explicit change of variables is then the
relabeling identifying the target's cut momenta with the source's; because
the measure is symmetric, any bijection matching the distinguished leg
works, and the canonical choice sends leg `k` to leg `k`.

The reduction is exact and symbolic: propagators are expanded over a Gram
matrix in the basis `{ka,kb,kc,ke,kf}` with
`ka^2 = kb^2 = kc^2 = 0`, `2 ka.kb = 1`, `2 ka.kc = v`, `2 kb.kc = w`, and
then reduced modulo the ideal generated by that family's three cut
conditions. No numerics.

## Normalization

The recorded period value is an exact **zero**, and a zero is invariant
under any nonzero normalization factor. The transfer of *this* period
therefore does not depend on the cross-family normalization constant — a
convenience that will not extend to a nonzero period.

The convention strings are recorded anyway, since a future nonzero transfer
must match them. All thirteen families involved carry the identical
convention modulo their own cut list:

```
dI/dv = Av.I at 2ka.kb=1, v=2ka.kc, w=2kb.kc; D=4-2eps; cuts {...}
```

## A defect found in the upstream period record

The transfer targets could not be located the obvious way. `NullityPeriods.wl`
records, per period,

```wolfram
"Families"  -> DeleteDuplicates[#["Family"] & /@ g],
"BlockRows" -> DeleteDuplicates[#["Rows"]   & /@ g]
```

(`scratchpad/nullity/dedup.wls` lines 72-73). The two lists are
deduplicated **independently**, so they are not positionally aligned
whenever two families share a row number. PID 6 lists 9 families but only 6
distinct `BlockRows` entries, and the family-to-row pairing is not
recoverable from the record at all.

Reading that record positionally is therefore wrong, and doing so initially
sent four of the PID-6 transfers (CF23, CF248, CF253, CF53) to unrelated
higher-sector masters with two or three uncut denominators, which then
looked like genuine shape mismatches.

`verify_transfers.wls` consequently does **not** use `BlockRows`. It
searches each target family's block basis for a master with the period's cut
shape and then checks the invariant signature. That is independent of the
damaged pairing and is a stronger statement: it decides whether the family
contains a realization of the period at all.

**Recommended upstream fix** (not applied here — the nullity counter is
outside this task's scope): store the pairing, e.g.
`"Realizations" -> DeleteDuplicates[{#["Family"], #["Rows"]} & /@ g]`,
rather than two independently deduplicated lists. Until then, no consumer
should read `Families` and `BlockRows` positionally.

## Scope limit

What is verified is that the target master is the *same cut integral* as the
source's, and that its differential-equation row has the same shape (coupling
only to its family's volume master). Combined with the exact zero proof for
the source, the target's period is exactly zero.

This does **not** by itself establish that the two families' *full* block
systems are equivalent, nor does it verify any nonzero period transfer. It
also does not revisit the nullity counter's upstream claim that these
families flag the period undetermined in the first place; it takes that list
as given and checks each entry on it.
