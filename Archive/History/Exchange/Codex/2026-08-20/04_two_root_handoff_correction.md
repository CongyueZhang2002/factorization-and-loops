The driver recognized only records written by the newest standardized-sector
directory.  It did not consult the exact Libra/blockwise records.  Therefore it
needlessly regenerated CF319 and CF321, began a duplicate CF385 calculation,
and would later have duplicated CF408.  A status message then counted only the
new directory and incorrectly reported 6/13 complete.  It also incorrectly
said that CF226 and CF249 required recertification.

The correct progression is:

- nine of thirteen complete before the latest sector campaign;
- CF254 completed afterward;
- ten of thirteen complete now;
- three of thirteen remain.

The duplicate CF385 process was stopped at final-sector strip (34,22).  Its
partial checkpoint is not a replacement for the existing exact CF385 result.
No calculation process from that campaign remains active.

## Reusable improvements retained

### Adaptive finite-field regulator sampling

For a strip reconstructed over several primes, the first prime retains the
full regulator sample set.  Its reconstructed numerator and denominator
degrees then determine the smaller sample sets for later primes.  If the
reduced schedule or the final exact lift fails, the calculation retries with
the original full schedule.

For CF321 strip (15,1), the measured affine-system count changed from

\[
  32+32+32=96
\]

to

\[
  32+14+14=60,
\]

while the exact two-variable Pfaffian identities remained zero.  This is a
37.5% reduction in affine finite-field solves for that strip.

### CANONICA candidate checks

Parallel CANONICA degree searches now return candidate gauges without each
worker repeating the full symbolic dlog identity.  The parent kernel performs
one exact identity check before accepting a candidate.  This changes the
location of the check, not its mathematical criterion.
