9 August 2026. The source was compiled three times to settle references. The
resulting 15 pages were rasterized at 120 dpi and inspected page by page.

Checked conditions:

- every displayed and inline equation is typeset from the retained LaTeX source;
- no equation, table, heading, footer, or page number is clipped;
- the two landscape inventory tables remain within their page boundaries;
- no missing glyph, undefined reference, LaTeX error, or overfull box appears in
  the final compiler log;
- notation is consistent between the derivation, timing discussion, and complete
  measurement inventory.

The final inspection also confirmed the repairs made during the render audit:

- the subsection 4.2 heading is separated from the Table 2 caption;
- explanatory paragraphs no longer collide with Tables 1, 3, or 5;
- the appendix inventory begins without an otherwise empty intervening page;
- all landscape tables fit inside the printable area and remain readable.

The rasterized inspection images are temporary quality-control material and are
not retained in the scientific record.
