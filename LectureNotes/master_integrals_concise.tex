\noindent
This is the compressed companion to the full lecture notes.  Setting:
the NNLO double-real channel of $pp \to h+X$; after reverse unitarity
and IBP reduction there are \textbf{347 master integrals} depending on
two Mandelstam ratios $(v,w)$, $0<v,w$, $v+w<1$, in $d=4-2\eps$.  The
goal: every master as an explicit $\eps$-expansion in iterated
integrals, with exact constants.

\section{Masters obey differential equations}
Differentiating a master with respect to $v$ or $w$ produces integrals
of the \emph{same family} (same propagator set), which IBP reduces
back to the masters of that family and its subsectors.  Hence per
family a closed first-order system
\begin{equation}
  \dd \vec I = A(v,w;\eps)\, \vec I,
  \qquad A = A_v \dd v + A_w \dd w,
\end{equation}
with rational $A$ and the integrability (\emph{flatness}) condition
$\dd A = A \wedge A$, certified exactly.  Families were fixed at
reduction time: \emph{canonical families} \texttt{CF}$n$ are
equivalence classes of propagator sets under loop-momentum shifts
($GL(2,\Z)$, cut lines to cut lines), detected via Pak's canonical
Symanzik form and verified by the explicit shift.  Differentiation is
family-local, so the 347 masters split into \textbf{91 independent
systems} (dimension 2--47).  Ordered by sectors each system is
\emph{block lower triangular}: diagonal blocks couple one sector
group's masters; off-diagonal blocks feed subsectors upward.

\section{Blocks and the 173 classes}
A \emph{block} is a strongly connected group of masters (a diagonal
block); the 91 systems contain 1119 of them.  A \emph{class} is an
equivalence class of blocks under basis permutation, optionally
composed with the crossing involution $v \leftrightarrow w$, as an
exact matrix identity --- there are only \textbf{173 classes}, because
the same subtopologies recur inside many parent families.  All hard
analytic work is done once per class, and a registry maps each class
solution back into every family that realizes it.

\section{The $\eps$-form and why it solves everything}
A system is in \emph{canonical ($\eps$-)form} if
\begin{equation}
  \dd \vec F = \eps \sum_a R_a\, \dd\log \varphi_a(v,w)\, \vec F,
  \qquad R_a\ \text{constant matrices},\quad
  \varphi_a\ \text{$\eps$-free \emph{letters}}.
\end{equation}
Expanding $\vec F = \sum_k \eps^k \vec F^{(k)}$ decouples the orders:
$\dd\vec F^{(k)} = \big(\sum_a R_a \dd\log\varphi_a\big)\vec
F^{(k-1)}$ --- each order is a quadrature of the previous one.  The
solution is the path-ordered exponential, i.e.\ order $\eps^k$ is a
sum of $k$-fold \emph{iterated integrals} (generalized
polylogarithms, GPLs) over the alphabet, of uniform weight $k$.
Example: letters $\{w, 1-w\}$ with nilpotent couplings generate
$\log(1-w)$ at order $\eps$ and $\mathrm{Li}_2(w)$ at order $\eps^2$.
The $\eps$-form thus converts coupled $\eps$-entangled PDEs into
explicit integrals; what remains is bookkeeping (paths, words) and
boundary constants.  Singularities are manifest: the connection's
poles are exactly the letters' zeros, and the residue matrix $R_a$
encodes the complete local behaviour there (exponents $=\eps\times$
eigenvalues, logs from Jordan blocks).

\section{Reaching the $\eps$-form}
Write the unknown basis change $T$: the connection transforms as
$A' = T^{-1}AT - T^{-1}\dd T$.  Three stages:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Diagonal blocks (per class).}  Rational-ansatz solvers
  and Fuchsification/balance reductions (\`a la Lee) produce a
  candidate $T$; acceptance is only the exact reconstruction of the
  stored form from constant residues.  Status: 173/173 classes done.
\item \textbf{Off-diagonal completion (per family).}  With canonical
  diagonal blocks $\eps e_k$, complete one block row at a time by a
  unipotent gauge $T = 1+D$ ($D^2=0$), which requires solving the
  \emph{linear} strip equation
  \begin{equation}
    \dd D_{kj} = \eps( e_k D_{kj} - D_{kj} c_j ) + \bar b_{kj}
    - \eps \textstyle\sum_a K_a \dd\log\varphi_a
  \end{equation}
  for a rational $D_{kj}$ and constant residues $K_a$.  Production
  method: ansatz over the letters with bounded numerator support,
  solved by finite-field sampling (exact linear algebra mod 31-bit
  primes), Chinese remaindering, rational reconstruction, held-out
  validation --- and always a final \emph{exact} identity as the
  certificate.  The solution's gauge freedom is fixed toward the
  low-degree representative (pinning top numerator coefficients), which
  controls degree growth down the rows.
\item \textbf{Constant $T(\eps)$.}  Leftover $\eps$-dependence in the
  residues stems from $\eps$-dependent basis normalizations and is
  removed by a kinematics-independent $T(\eps)$ found from sampled
  residue matrices and verified symbolically.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Roots, charts, Galois}
Phase-space geometry injects square roots: the 3-particle boundary is
the K\"all\'en polynomial $\lambda_1 = (1-v-w)^2 - 4vw$, and alphabets
of many blocks contain $\sqrt{\lambda_1}$-type letters.  The
\emph{number of roots} of a family = independent square classes among
its radicands (44 families root-free, 31 one-root, 13 two-root, 3
three-root).  A \emph{chart} is a rational substitution making a root
rational: e.g.\ $v = xy$, $w=(1-x)(1-y)$ gives $\lambda_1 = (x-y)^2$
(chart \textsf{Kallen1}); catalog charts \textsf{Kallen1/2/3},
\textsf{Q4a/b}, \textsf{Bilinear115} cover the single roots, and
iterated conic parametrization gives joint charts
\textsf{Kallen12/13/23} for the two-root families, whose $\eps$-forms
live in chart variables.  For three roots no chart exists (the joint
cover is a K3 surface); instead one computes in the multiquadratic
\emph{Galois algebra} $\Q(v,w,\eps)[r_1,r_2,r_3]/(r_i^2-q_i)$, whose
$(\Z/2)^3$ sign group grades all functions into eight channels ---
linear algebra runs on the rational channel components, and the eight
sign branches recombine as a certificate.  This is the current
frontier (\texttt{CF259/300/303}).

\section{Transport}
Solving in practice = choosing a base point and paths in the physical
triangle, restricting letters to each segment (kept linear in the path
parameter), and accumulating GPL words order by order.  Flatness makes
the result path-independent.  The $\eps$-depth per master is budgeted
in advance from where it enters the assembled cross section (203
masters need the finite part only, 131 one pole deeper, 9 more);
chart families transport in their chart variables.

\section{Boundary constants}
The general solution leaves one constant vector per family per
$\eps$-order.  Almost none are new numbers; constraints are applied in
order:
(1) \emph{volume rows} --- the phase-space volume master is a closed-form
$\Gamma$-ratio, seeding the lattice;
(2) \emph{inherited rows} --- subsector masters already solved elsewhere
constrain each family (families processed in dependency order);
(3) \emph{forbidden modes} --- near $w\to0$, $v\to0$, and the soft
surface $1-v-w\to0$ the integral \emph{representation} allows only
certain exponents $n + a\eps$ (a power-counting statement per
stratum, computed per chart), while the DE offers modes with $a$ from
residue eigenvalues; every representation-forbidden mode kills one
constant;
(4) \emph{regularity} at letter zeros interior to an integral's
physical region.
The number of genuinely new constants per block is the \emph{nullity}
$\dim\ker C$ of the assembled constraint matrix, computed before
evaluating anything; inventory-wide the surviving \emph{boundary
periods} are a short list of cut phase-space integrals at ordered
limits, each established by an exact, numerics-free proof chain (with
independent numerics as a guide only).

\section{Endpoint behaviour: don't expand it away}
Near an endpoint the solution is a sum of modes $u^{\,n+a\eps}(\log
u)^j$ with data read exactly off the residue matrices (this is ``the
singularity is in the matrix'').  For the cross section one needs
the distributional expansion
\begin{equation}
  (1-w)^{-1+a\eps} = \frac{1}{a\eps}\,\delta(1-w)
  + \sum_{k\ge0}\frac{(a\eps)^k}{k!}
  \Big[\frac{\log^k(1-w)}{1-w}\Big]_+ ,
\end{equation}
which exists only for the \emph{unexpanded} power --- expanding the
master in $\eps$ first destroys the $\delta$ content.  Masters
therefore ship with exact indicial data alongside their GPL
expansions, and endpoint extraction consumes the modes.

\section{Certification}
Every stage pairs a free-form search (ansatz, modular, numeric) with
an exact certificate independent of the search: flatness identities,
exact reconstruction of class forms, the exact family gauge identity
(symbolically or by modular evaluation with held-out primes), and
numerics-free proof chains for periods.  The final artifact --- 347
masters as certified $\eps$-forms plus boundary data --- verifies
itself.

\paragraph{Vocabulary in one breath.}  \emph{Family}: propagator set
mod momentum shifts (\texttt{CF}$n$).  \emph{Sector}: propagator
subset.  \emph{Block}: strongly connected master group = diagonal
block.  \emph{Class}: block mod permutation $\circ$
($v\leftrightarrow w$).  \emph{Connection}: $A$ in $\dd\vec I=A\vec
I$.  \emph{$\eps$-form}: $A=\eps\sum R_a\dd\log\varphi_a$, constant
$R_a$.  \emph{Letter/alphabet}: the $\varphi_a$/their set.
\emph{Strip}: an off-diagonal block row being completed.
\emph{Root}: independent square class $\sqrt{q}$ in an alphabet.
\emph{Chart}: rational substitution rationalizing roots.
\emph{Transport}: building the path-ordered solution.
\emph{Period}: genuinely new boundary constant.  \emph{Nullity}:
$\dim\ker(\text{constraints})$ = number of new periods.
\emph{Indicial data}: exponents/log powers at singular loci from
residue matrices.
